# Code of conduct

hamstack follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(the text is available under CC BY 4.0). In short:

## Our pledge

We want participation to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or identity and orientation.
Amateur radio is a hobby that welcomes newcomers and old hands alike, and so should this project.

## Standards

Things that make a good community: being kind and patient with questions, giving and gracefully accepting
constructive feedback, owning mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the project and the people using it.

Things that are not acceptable: sexualized language or attention, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments,
personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, and publishing other people's private information
(a home address or a callsign tied to a person who has not shared it, for example) without permission.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions) and wherever someone represents the project.
Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not follow this code, and may restrict participation.

To report a problem, use the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form or contact a maintainer
through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately and in good faith.
